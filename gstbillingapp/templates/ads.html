Why a customer's balance and your ledger disagree

Nine times in ten it isn't arithmetic, it's timing: a cheque written on the 28th and banked on the 2nd sits in two different months. Agree the date you count it before you argue about the number, and most disputes end there.

Collection day beats collection effort

A fixed day per route is worth more than any reminder. The shop keeps your money ready because it knows you are coming, and your rep stops making decisions about who to visit.

What to do with a cheque that bounces twice

Stop supplying before you stop asking. A second bounce is information about the shop's cash position, not about their intentions — supplying through it turns a recoverable amount into a bad debt.
